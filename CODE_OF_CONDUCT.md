# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone who contributes to or participates in the OpenSelfHosting Skills community, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while taking reported harm seriously.
- Accept feedback and focus on what benefits the community.
- Respect privacy and do not publish secrets or private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the official GitHub organization contact path. Maintainers should review reports fairly, protect the privacy of reporters where possible, and take proportionate action. This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public spaces.

This code of conduct is adapted from common open-source community practice. A future release may adopt a named standard and version through a maintainer decision.
